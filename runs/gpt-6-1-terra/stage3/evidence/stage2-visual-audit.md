# Phase 2 visual audit

A browser DOM measurement at 1440 × 900 found the chassis at x=57.59, y=259.98, size 1324.80 × 428.02 px: 92.0% of viewport width and aspect ratio 3.095. The deck and keybed measured 225.72 and 192.28 px respectively (54.0% / 46.0% of their combined height). Section widths were 174.64, 249.53, 106.05, 155.95, 311.89 and 249.52 px. The document width was 1440 px and all 111 panel control centers passed a hit test.

At 390 × 844, the chassis was x=12, y=226.17, 1000 × 323.08 px inside a horizontal scroll container; document width remained 390 px. These are browser-tool measurements, not seal-harness captures. The operator's harness supplies the canonical PNGs and capture JSON.

The first Phase 2 layout placed all supplemental settings above the chassis, moving it down and crowding narrow screens. Collapsing these settings into an accessible disclosure restored the desktop chassis bounds to the Phase 1 measurement. Narrow inspection still requires horizontal scrolling. The 73-key E2–E8 pattern and six section widths are unchanged.

Known deviations: the Phase 1 surface remains an approximation and omits or simplifies physical controls and legends. Supplemental layer and effect controls are in the disclosure rather than on a photo-exact panel. Audio algorithms are approximations, particularly the synthesized piano voices, amp models, modulation, rotary and resonance; samples in the extreme upper register are transposed beyond the source's highest recorded note. Organ, Synth, Program and excluded controls remain decorative. No seal-time images were written by this audit.
