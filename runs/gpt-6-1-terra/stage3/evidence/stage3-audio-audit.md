# Phase 3 audio audit — in progress

A Chrome browser ran `tests/stage2-audio-browser.mjs` against the expanded backend successfully, including piano sources, effects and cleanup. `tests/stage3-audio-browser.mjs` rendered the new Organ and Synth voices through the same backend. The four required Organ models produced four distinct signatures; all 14 named Synth sources produced distinct signatures, and the tested category controls changed the output while Pure Saw ignored Osc Ctrl. Representative filter, drive, envelope, drawbar, percussion, vibrato, voice-mode and effect-target settings changed rendered output. A zero master produced zero output, Synth A effects were isolated from Organ and Synth B, and a real AudioContext cleanup check returned zero active or held voices.

The program/split/morph audio check feeds serialized state and calculated gains into an offline render; it does not exercise the full UI-to-audio path. An injected-clock arpeggiator test checked the sequence C4, E4, C5, E5 and rendered distinct notes. The UI program, Live, split, scene and morph tests exercise state interactions separately. These are partial checks, not full acceptance: all morph destinations, continuous modulation of all held voices, clock subdivisions and transitions, per-layer routing and sustain, hardware feedback, and comprehensive rendered envelope/voice/arp combinations need further verification. Organ and Synth are generated with Web Audio, not recorded samples.

The latest additional checks also exercised the five LFO shapes and three destinations, separate Organ/Synth sustain release, and Organ rotary slow/fast/stop routing. These checks passed but do not cover the known deviations in the binding audit.
