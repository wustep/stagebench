# Phase 3 binding audit — in progress

The surface exposes program slot/page/dial/Store/Live, split, scene, morph, transpose/Panic and Master Clock controls. The expanded settings supply naming and destinations, split positions and widths, zone ranges, pedal and clock controls. The surface exposes Organ model, percussion, chorus selection, drawbars, levels, layer focus/enable, octave and rotary routing; the settings supply additional controls. Synth waveform, filter, LFO, envelope, arp, levels, layer focus/enable and octave are bound, with remaining settings in the expanded panel. Effect focus selects the Organ chain or one of the Synth chains, alongside the existing Piano chains.

The only controls still labeled decorative on the surface are Piano Model (one model per type), Aftertouch (spec-excluded), Oscillator Mode (Analog only), and Oscillator Shape (optional category not implemented). The in-app unsupported notes list the spec exclusions. The numeric settings panels and the hardware controls do not yet provide the precise manual interaction and feedback for every function. In particular, the implementation uses simplified voice and modulation models, and some continuous control updates, rotary/morph feedback, per-layer pedal and clock behavior require more validation. This is not a completed control-by-control acceptance audit.

Known deviations still needing implementation or verification: Synth legato now retunes a held voice without retriggering its envelope; its glide timing still needs broader validation. Wheel vibrato is updated on held Synth voices, but its response and menu parameters need broader validation. Oscillator-control modulation for Sync uses a periodic-wave approximation. Arp Poly/Gate and clock timing have basic implementations but lack full subdivision, hold and transition coverage. The program and morph tests cover representative snapshots and destinations, not an exhaustive UI-to-audio round trip for all supported settings. The button-latched assignment and clock-dial interaction approximates the hardware hold gesture. These gaps prevent a complete Phase 3 acceptance claim.
