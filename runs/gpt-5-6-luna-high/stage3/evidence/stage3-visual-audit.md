# Phase 3 visual and interaction audit

The desktop and narrow captures preserve the verified Phase 2 73-key chassis geometry (continuous red shell, 54/46 deck/keybed allocation, 13/21/15/9/21/21 section ratios and 43 white + 30 black keys). Program and Synth remain the only primary OLEDs. The Program OLED now renders canonical name/category/list/dirty state; the Effects header renders active unit wet/dry state and the bottom rail renders split points, active scene and morph count.

Exercised flows: Store/Store As, dirty indicator, Cancel and Undo, Live slots 1–8, Scene I/II, Low/Mid/High split cycling, 0/6/12 crossfade cycling, and Wheel/Aftertouch/Control Pedal morph assignment. Effects graph checks exercised Mod 1/2, Delay, Amp/EQ, Compressor, Reverb and Rotary processing, bypass and all-effects bypass; Piano rapid note play continued through the shared graph.

Console state: no application errors observed in deterministic test/browser setup. Organ and Synth controls remain visual-only as required until Phase 4; their buses are provisioned but no audible engine claim is made.

Remaining deviations: (1) effect controls remain compact representations of the full manual inventory, (2) visual captures inherit the simplified product-study materials and typography, and (3) Organ/Synth remain visual-only until Phase 4 while their buses are reserved.
