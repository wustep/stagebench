# Phase 3 visual and interaction audit

The inherited Stage 4 73 chassis keeps its 73 E-to-E keybed, six proportional control-deck sections, and the two primary OLED locations. Phase 3 replaces the former presentation-only Program, Organ, and Synth interaction banks without adding a display or changing the chassis geometry.

Exercised flows: a named Store As to a regular slot, edit indicator and discard on another program selection, Live mode, split points/crossfade, Scene I/II, morph source/clear, clock/transpose/Panic, Organ model/drawbar/percussion/rotary, and Synth waveform/filter/LFO/arp state. At narrow width the instrument retains its intentional horizontal inspection mode inherited from Phase 1 rather than clipping its dense deck.

Known limitation: the supplied isolated candidate contains no recorded piano assets or browser capture runner. The UI truthfully labels its live generated-synthesis fallback; parent-controlled sealing captures the built artifact.
