# Bundled piano recordings

Grand: **Salamander Grand Piano V3**, Yamaha C5, recorded by **Alexander Holm**. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano . Licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Selected recorded velocities 4, 9, 14; minor-third root notes. Original FLAC files, no modifications.

Upright: **Upright Piano KW**, Kawai upright, recorded by **Gonzalo and Roberto** for **FreePats** (January 2017). Source: https://github.com/freepats/upright-piano-KW . Dedicated under **CC0 1.0**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Original FLAC files, no modifications. Low/high recorded velocities; nearest-root selection.

Electric: **Wurlitzer EP200 Electric Piano**, recorded by **Greg Sullivan**, SFZ/FLAC mapping by **kinwie**. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos . Licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Original pp/mp/f/ff FLAC files, no modifications. Web sampler selects nearest root within the recorded velocity band rather than reproducing the SFZ mapping verbatim.

Each model directory contains the original publisher LICENSE and README. `manifest.json` enumerates every file, root, recorded velocity interval, pinned publisher URL, SHA256 and size. This asset subset is derived from the named publisher instruments. The source code license does not replace these asset licenses.

Clav, Digital, Misc, simulated resonance and the labeled failure fallback are original generated PCM under the candidate MIT license. They are not recorded samples or Nord samples.
