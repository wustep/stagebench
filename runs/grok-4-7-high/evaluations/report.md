### Priority issues

- **major** — All-effects bypass leaves the To Rotary routing active: With Amp Sim/EQ set to 'To Rotary', neither the All FX Off pulse nor toggling Layer Effects On restores the dry signal. Measured at the destination: dry energy 3.09e-3; effects on 9.42e-2; after all-fx-off 5.54e-2; after toggling Layer Effects On off 7.03e-2 with centroid 2387 Hz vs 935 Hz dry. The effects spec requires the Layer Effects ON button to bypass every effect at once. Evidence: scratch/probe-bypass-v1.mjs.
- **minor** — Reverb decay order wrong between Room and Booth: The spec says decay grows from Booth (very short) to Cathedral. Measured tail energy over the six reverb types is Room 3.75e-4, Booth 7.36e-4, Spring 3.19e-4, Stage 2.15e-3, Hall 5.49e-3, Cathedral 1.18e-2 (scratch/fxtypes.txt), so Booth rings about twice as long as Room. Every type is individually distinct; only the ordering at the short end is off.
- **minor** — Soft Release and String Res collapsed into one mutually exclusive cycle: artifact/src/model/hardware.ts:168 defines piano-acoustics with cycleLabels ['Off','Soft Rel','String Res'], so the two acoustics features cannot be engaged together, unlike the Stage 4 which has separate switches (visible in inputs/reference/nord-stage-4-73.jpg). Both are measurable individually (Soft Rel rms 0.01896 vs 0.01534 off; String Res under sustain rms 0.034 vs 0.0372), but String Res attenuates rather than adding sympathetic energy. - **minor** — Piano Model dial is inert and undeclared: Driving piano-model across its whole 0-127 range leaves the Program OLED at 'Salamander Grand' and the rendered energy unchanged (3.22e-3 vs 3.25e-3, scratch/probe-reliability-ee1.mjs). - **minor** — MIDI denial and absence degrade silently: With navigator.requestMIDIAccess rejected, and also with it deleted entirely, nothing on the panel reports it: the OLED still reads 'STAGE 4 73 / PIANO IDLE / tap a key to play' and the only signal is the DOM attribute data-midi='denied' (scratch/probe-midivis-gg1.mjs).
