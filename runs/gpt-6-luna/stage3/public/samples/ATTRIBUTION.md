# Bundled piano recordings

Selected FLAC sources were converted to mono Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz and trimmed to at most six seconds. The complete converted inventory is in `library.json`.

- **Grand Piano:** Salamander Grand Piano V3, Alexander Holm. [Source](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano). [CC BY 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); attribution required.
- **Upright Piano:** Upright Piano KW Kawai, Gonzalo and Roberto. [Source](https://github.com/freepats/upright-piano-KW). [CC0 1.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Electric Piano:** jRhodes3d, Jeffrey Learman. [Source](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d). [CC BY-NC 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/); redistribution requires attribution and is restricted to noncommercial use.
