## Surface and interaction inspection

- Performance has exposed red metal, master knob, a separate rotary-control stack, pitch stick, modulation wheel and branding.
- Organ has two layer faders with LED ladders, nine physical drawbars with LED ladders and differentiated brown/ivory/black caps, model, vibrato and percussion switches.
- Piano has two layer faders, model/type/timbre and detail controls, with printed legends and no OLED.
- Program has the primary OLED, dial, eight numbered program buttons, page navigation, morph assignments, scene/live/store/split controls. Static OLED text explicitly labels the panel decorative.
- Synth has one compact OLED (29% of its body width), three layer faders and visually distinct oscillator, arp/voice, LFO/filter/envelope groups.
- Effects has five separated vertical strips, compressor and EQ controls, and layer-focus buttons. It has no OLED.
- 155 uniquely identified physical panel inputs are rendered. All pass center-point hit testing and real browser keyboard/pointer interaction. Every input has an accessible name, value or pressed state, and a visible focus treatment. Their state does not initialize or modify the audio engine.
- Desktop has no vertical scroll. Narrow view shows the whole silhouette without clipping or page overflow. Inspect surface enlarges it to 1400 px in a horizontally scrollable local viewport; the rightmost effects controls remain reachable, and Fit instrument restores the overview.

## Corrections made during verification

The initial inventory normalized Unicode drawbar fractions into duplicate IDs; explicit fraction names now give all nine drawbars unique stable IDs. Overlapping organ top-row placements were separated. The initial white-key shading made keys appear concave; the final build uses a flatter ivory face and localized top/bottom shading. The final browser run rechecks the resulting build before taking canonical images.

## Known deviations and limits

This is a CSS/DOM reconstruction, not a pixel-identical tracing. Micro-legends are abbreviated and some small control placements, the logo typeface, bevels, rotary enclosure and rear connectors are simplified. The surfaces approximate red painted metal, blue-gray inset panels and black indexed knobs. No product photograph is rendered as a background or used to replace interactive hardware.

The narrow fit view is an overview, with labels too small for close inspection until enlarged. Native browser page zoom also works. UI controls outside the chassis provide audio activation, sustain, stop, inspection and MIDI connection; these are explicitly separate from the decorative hardware.

One original generated piano-like voice is the only audio source. There are no recordings, working panel programs/effects, Organ/Synth engines or sampled-piano fallback. Eight-second natural decay and 220 ms damper release are documented in IMPLEMENTATION_DETAILS.json. Tests exercise denied/disconnected MIDI through injection; no physical MIDI device is claimed to have been used.
