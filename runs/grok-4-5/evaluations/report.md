## Phase 1: Complete surface and basic piano

**70/100**

A technically clean, honest Phase 1 whose panel does not fit on its own chassis. Audio is the strong half: one additive four-partial voice, correct pitch E1 (46.9 Hz) to E7 (2636.7 Hz), velocity on pointer (2.04x) and MIDI (3.2x), Space and CC64 sustain, a hard 32-voice ceiling with oldest-first stealing (live oscillators plateau at 128), all-notes-off on blur, node counts back to zero. Pointer, multi-touch, computer keyboard and Web MIDI all reach that one lifecycle. The declared source is what the build does - no audio requests, no decodeAudioData - and 'panel controls are decorative' survives measurement: master level at 0 and Piano Section off leave the strike peak unchanged at 0.06563. The failure is layout: the deck gets 0.476 of instrument height instead of 0.54, so 64 of 161 controls render under the keybed and are clipped away entirely (piano shows 4 of 24, synth 11 of 38); a 5x5 elementFromPoint grid reaches 87 of 161. The visual audit then lists those invisible controls as landmarks exercised and reports the 0.54/0.46 split as achieved, which is where an otherwise scrupulous artifact stops being accurate about itself. The voice also never decays - flat RMS 0.0201 across a one-second hold.

## Phase 2: Piano library and working effects

**43/100**

Measured on build/ with a tap patched onto AudioNode.prototype.connect at the destination. The skeleton is right: one AudioContext, per-layer buses, six units in the documented order, one edge into the destination through master gain + limiter, per-unit bypass that restores dry exactly (rmsRatio 1.009, harmDist 0.008), working layers/octave/master level, real MIDI velocity and CC64, and exact cleanup after a 99-note stress burst. The audio is where it breaks. All 54 bundled WAVs are malformed (audioFormat=257, numChannels=0), so no sample ever decodes and the build runs the labeled fallback synth permanently; Grand and Upright then differ by ~1 dB on one partial. To Rotary routes past the layer-level node, leaving the Piano Level fader inert (0.985x vs 0.301x dry). Mod 1 sums all six type paths in parallel, so every type adds ~2x gain and Wah peaks at 2.32 full scale. The compressor widens crest factor 8.1 to 14.3. Timbre is inert in 4 of 6 settings. 74 of 161 controls fail the 5x5 elementFromPoint grid at 1440x900. None of it was caught because every audio test measures engine.measureEnergy(), a stand-in that under jsdom collapses to arithmetic on type-name character codes.
