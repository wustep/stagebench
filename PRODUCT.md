# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and evaluators running long-horizon coding-agent benchmarks locally. They arrive from a terminal or active agent session and need to see which model ran, how far it progressed, and whether an output is ready to inspect.

## Product Purpose

Stagebench runs and compares incremental Nord Stage 4 web recreations. The main page is a compact run index: it should make benchmark state legible at a glance and provide direct access to completed outputs without becoming a marketing site for the benchmark itself.

## Brand Personality

Precise, utilitarian, unmistakably Nord. The interface should feel like a well-labeled instrument panel: confident red hardware, restrained black and white controls, short factual language, and no theatrical sales copy.

## Anti-references

- Marketing landing pages with oversized slogans, multiple promotional sections, and repeated calls to action.
- Generic cream editorial layouts, faux paper texture, ornamental grids, and decorative section numbering.
- Dark SaaS dashboards, floating glass cards, and visual effects unrelated to benchmark state.
- Keyboard illustrations that use only black control decks and miss the Nord Stage's dominant red chassis.

## Design Principles

1. Put runs first: current model and phase status are the primary content.
2. Resemble the instrument through structure and color, not decorative imitation.
3. Say each thing once in plain language.
4. Keep benchmark history honest: official, exploratory, legacy, partial, and invalid runs stay visible in distinct groups.
5. Make every interaction familiar, keyboard-accessible, and quick.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve visible focus states, complete keyboard access, semantic status text in addition to color, comfortable touch targets, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
