# Sample credits

- Grand: AKAI Splendid Grand samples, described by the [SFZ Instruments mirror](https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-splendid-grand-piano) as public domain. Ogg Opus encoding is a derivative of the mirror's samples.
- Upright: FreePats Kawai KW, recorded by Gonzalo and Roberto, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The bundled mono Ogg Opus derivatives are truncated to nine seconds. Source: https://github.com/freepats/upright-piano-KW.
- Electric: Wurlitzer EP200 recorded by Greg Sullivan, [CC BY 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), sourced from https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-greg-sullivan-e-pianos. The mirror converts the original recordings to Ogg Opus; `ab6mp.ogg` was converted locally from the original FLAC because the mirror copy could not be decoded.

Exact bundled files, root notes, velocity labels and SHA-256 hashes are in `IMPLEMENTATION_DETAILS.json` and `src/samples.json`.
