Visual fidelity

CriterionRatingScoreEvidence
Silhouette and proportions 3/4 75
  • evidence/stage1-desktop.png shows a continuous red chassis, end cheeks, top rail, bottom rail, and complete keybed fully inside the 1440x900 viewport at approximately 94% width.
  • IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md and styles.css encode the measured 3.0951 aspect ratio and 54/46 control-deck/keybed allocation; the 43-white/30-black E-to-E keyboard is visibly complete.
  • Compared with the reference crop, the control deck is still more vertically open and the rails/cheeks are simplified, so this is strong but not exceptional fidelity.
Panel layout and density 2/4 50
  • The desktop render has the correct six-section order and 13/21/15/9/21/21 grid, with clear Performance, Organ, Piano, Program/Morph, Synth, and Layer Effects boundaries.
  • sectionDefs contains section-specific drawbars, piano selectors, program navigation/live controls, synth oscillator/filter/envelope/LFO/arp controls, and effect controls.
  • Most of those controls are hidden by .panel-content overflow in the short deck (for example Organ/Piano/Program), and the visible density remains far below the reference photograph's tightly packed hardware.
Control materials and color 2/4 50
  • styles.css provides red chassis, slate inset panels, metallic gradients for knobs/faders/drawbars, key shading, and teal OLED treatment close to the reference palette.
  • The render lacks the reference's many discrete LED ladders, silver toggle hardware, varied drawbar caps, real pitch stick/mod wheel geometry, and dense material micro-detail; performance pitch/mod are vertical range sliders.
Typography and displays 2/4 50
  • Only Program and Synth render OLED elements, and the Synth display changes from SYNTH / WAVE to SYNTH / ARP when Arpeggiator is toggled; no forbidden Organ/Piano/Effects OLED is present.
  • Section headings use a condensed display face and the required Nord Stage 4 caption/branding is present, but the photograph's tiny legends, display framing, program metadata, and typography hierarchy are largely omitted.
