Phase 2

Piano library and working effects

Audio strategy
Phase 2 has three recorded, bundled, offline sample sets (Grand, Upright, Electric) played through per-note sample voices, plus three honestly synthesized types (Clav, Digital, Misc) and the Phase 1 live-synthesis voice as a labelled fallback. Phase 3

Complete Stage 4 system

Audio strategy
Phase 3 completes the instrument on one AudioContext. The three recorded, bundled, offline piano sample sets (Grand, Upright, Electric) are unchanged from Phase 2. Every other sound is live synthesis, declared in generatedSources: the organ (four models built from sine partials, percussion, key click, vibrato and chorus), the synth (fourteen required waveforms, filters, envelopes, LFO, voice modes), and the Phase 2 piano synthesis and fallback.   • Organ tone engines (B3, Vox, Farf, Pipe 1, Pipe 2) — Sine partials per drawbar, one rule per model: B3 linear per drawbar on 9 footages; Vox flat over 7 partials with the ninth drawbar dulling the upper partials; Farf on/off registers with odd-weighted partials; Pipe 1 a 1/ratio rank series; Pipe 2 the Pipe 1 engine with a brighter 1/ratio^0.7 slope (reuses the Pipe 1 engine, as the spec allows). Generated at note-on. Not recordings.
  • B3 percussion (2nd or 3rd harmonic, single-triggered) — One sine partial at the second or third harmonic with a linear decay of 0.25 s (fast) or 0.9 s (slow), level by soft/normal and the volume knob. Retriggers only when the layer is silent (manual p. 20).

  • Vibrato and chorus (LFO detune) — A 6.1 Hz sine LFO drives the detune of every partial (V1 to V3, 10 to 35 cents at full depth). C1 to C3 mix a pitch-modulated copy of the partials with the dry set at 25 to 65 percent.

Multi and Super: stacks of detuned saws or squares, spread by Osc Ctrl. Sync: a second oscillator at a relative pitch of 1 to 4 times, a documented approximation of hard sync (no oscillator reset). FM 2-op: a sine carrier with a 2x modulator whose depth is Osc Ctrl.
  • White noise (generated loop) — A two-second loop of deterministic white noise, generated once per context. Not a recording.
  • Synth filters, envelopes, and LFO — LP12, LP24 (two cascaded low-pass stages), HP, and BP biquad filters with keyboard tracking, resonance, and a soft-clip drive stage. Amplifier, filter, and oscillator envelopes built from scheduled ramps; a finite decay settles at 0.7 (amplifier) or 0.5 (filter and oscillator) of its peak, and a maximum decay holds at the peak. LFO: triangle, two saws, square, and a Sample and Hold shape, which is a fixed pseudo-random periodic wave (deterministic, not a stepped random sample).
  • Arpeggiator sequencer (note events, no audio source) — A deterministic sequencer that schedules note-on and note-off events on a fixed step grid from the clock. It has no audio of its own; its notes are played by the synth layers.

Notes
  • Sample files live in public/samples/ and are fetched relative to the page, so the build works offline from any folder (base './'). Nothing is fetched from the network at runtime.
  • The sample sets were built by tools/samples/build_piano_samples.py from the three upstream archives. The script is the provenance record for every file.
  • Unison, Dyn Comp, KB Touch, Timbre, Soft Release, and Master Level act on the rendered signal through gains, curves, and filters; they are not separate recordings.
  • Organ, synth, and program state are serializable: a program carries every supported setting, split, scene, morph, clock value, and transpose. Master Level, the performance inputs, and the bank's own selection are not saved in a program.
  • Declared gaps (Phase 3). Organ: B3 Bass is not offered (optional, and the panel has no switch for it); the vibrato and chorus on switch is shared by both layers, not per layer; the Vox mix drawbar is an approximation (it dulls partials at ratio 4 and up); the swell pedal, rotary stop mode, and the preset and drawbar-live modes are excluded by the spec. Synth: Samples mode, LP M, LP+HP, Sub Osc, Shape, Misc, Wave, FM inharmonic, Sound Init, the pattern editor, zig-zag, accent, pan, and KB Hold are not implemented (optional or excluded). Hard sync is approximated by a detuned pair; the Sample and Hold LFO is a fixed periodic wave; the LFO Osc Ctrl destination scales the stack's detune; Vibrato in Wheel mode reads the wheel at note-on. Arpeggiator gate length is fixed at half a step, and the Gate mode does not repurpose the range knob. Mod 1 syncs to the clock for A-Pan and Tremolo only; the other Mod 1 types are unsynced. Rotary speed is a continuous control (Slow at 0, Fast at 1), so it can be morphed. Sustain follows the piano's SUSTPED switch for all engines (no per-section SUSTPED). Store: while STORE is armed, the program buttons choose the destination without auditioning it, so the edits being stored are kept. Program: banks beyond one, Organize, categories, sorting, Solo, Prog View, and single-level undo are not implemented. Aftertouch is excluded: the A.T. morph source is decorative. The piano model dial stays decorative (one model per type).
