Audio and sound samples

Audio strategy
Generated live synthesis (no recorded samples). Each note builds a small stack of detuned oscillator partials (triangle fundamental plus sine partials at 1x, 2x and ~3x) through a velocity- and pitch-keyed lowpass filter and a percussive exponential-decay gain envelope, into a shared master gain and DynamicsCompressor soft limiter, then one destination. Piano-like by design and honestly not a sampled piano.
