  • Because organ/synth effects are baked at note-on, no effect parameter, layer level or rotary state can alter a sounding organ/synth note; only piano parameters ramp live (rack.level and master.gain use linearRampToValueAtTime). Focus/group/global routing state itself is correct: clicking Piano B moves effects focus to piano/B, Synth focus to synth/A-C.
Musical quality under load 2/4 50
  • Polyphony and stability are sound: 12 simultaneous computer-keyboard notes report '12 voices', rendered peak 0.6375 with no clipping (limiter threshold -2 dB, ratio 18), AudioContext stays 'running', zero console errors across every probe session, and a 3-note chord shows all three fundamentals (129/164/196 Hz) - no voice stealing at that size.
  • Latency is the failure: each note-on synchronously renders a full 2.4 s buffer per enabled layer on the main thread. Measured main-thread block for the app's own keydown handler: 28.5 ms piano only, 52.6 ms piano+organ, 72.2 ms piano+organ+synth, 159.5 ms with all seven layers enabled. 40 rapid note on/off pairs took 6773 ms against 1200 ms of scheduled waiting.
  • The AudioContext survives panel interaction (state 'running' after dozens of clicks and range moves) and a master-level move mid-note is honoured live, so there is no context-close or rebuild-per-note defect.

Organ system 2/4 50
  • Drawbars, models, percussion and key click all reach audio.
