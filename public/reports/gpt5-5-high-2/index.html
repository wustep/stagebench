Complete Synth system 2/4 50
  • stage3System.ts defines three synth layers, Pure/Sync/Multi/Super/FM-H waveform lists, oscillator control, LP12/LP24/HP/BP filtering, envelopes, LFO waveforms, voice modes, and arpeggiatorSteps; tests render distinct probe outputs for these.
