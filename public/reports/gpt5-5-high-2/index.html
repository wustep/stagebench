Voice lifecycle and performance 3/4 75
  • src/pianoEngine.ts implements velocity-scaled gain, per-note release envelopes, sustain hold/release, maxVoices=18 in the app, deterministic stealing preferring released/oldest voices, allNotesOff, and dispose cleanup.
